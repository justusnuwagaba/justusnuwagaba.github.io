Questions

Is WordCare free?

Yes. Every feature is free, including merging, compressing, signing and locking PDFs, scanning and editing Office files. The free version shows a small ad; an optional one-time purchase removes it.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and only used for backup to your own Google Drive.

Does WordCare work offline?

Yes. Scanning, text recognition, PDF tools and Office editing all run on your phone without internet.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on backup to your own Google Drive.

Can WordCare scan both sides of an ID card onto one page?

Yes. Choose ID card in the scanner, scan the front and then the back, and WordCare puts both sides on one page at real card size.

Can it scan a book?

Yes. Choose Book, photograph the open book, and WordCare splits the two facing pages into separate pages.

Can I edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint files?

Yes. WordCare opens and edits .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files, with formulas that calculate in spreadsheets and full-screen presenting for slides.

Is there an iPhone version?

The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store. The Android version is available on Google Play now.
